# Craps — Support

Craps is a play-money dice game for iPhone and iPad. It does not offer
real-money wagering, prizes, purchases, accounts, advertising, or tracking.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, or questions, email
[Justin@audioplane.com](mailto:Justin@audioplane.com).

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS
version, and a short description of what happened.

Read the [Craps Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).

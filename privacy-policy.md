# Privacy Policy — Craps

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

Thank you for playing Craps ("the App"). This policy explains how the App
handles information. In short: **the App does not collect, store, or share any
personal information.**

## Information we collect

**None.** The App does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal data. It
has no account system, no login, and no sign-up.

- We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts.
- We do **not** collect location data.
- We do **not** use analytics or advertising SDKs.
- We do **not** track you across apps or websites.

## Game state

Your play-money bankroll and current game state exist only while the App is
running. The App does not save your bankroll, bets, or game history to persistent
storage, and the current game resets after the App process closes.

## Money

The App is for entertainment only. It uses **play money with no real-world
value**. There are no in-app purchases, no real-money wagering, and no way to
win or cash out real money or prizes.

## Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children. It is rated 18+ because it depicts frequent simulated gambling; it
involves no real gambling and no data collection.

## Third parties

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking
services, and does not share data with third parties.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a
new "Last updated" date.
